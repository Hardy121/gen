The city hums beneath a lid of glass,
Where neon ripples in the heavy air,
And midnight shadows flicker as they pass
Across the quiet square.

A sudden draft disturbs the fallen leaves,
They spiral upward in a brief embrace,
Before the wind forgets the shape it weaves
And leaves an empty place.

Somewhere a clock counts downward to the dawn,
A steady pulse against the shifting dark,
While in the stillness, waiting to be drawn,
The morning leaves its mark.
